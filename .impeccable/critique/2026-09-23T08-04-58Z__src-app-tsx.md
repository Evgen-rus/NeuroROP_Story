## Overall Impression

The opening and movement from team to deal are clear and credible. The strongest opportunity is to make the final third earn trust and end in a real, approved next step.

## What's Working

- The light, spacious presentation and semantic traffic light make team priorities easy to scan.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Final action reaches a dead end.** `src/chapters/SceneFinal.tsx:21–43` uses a different CTA than the approved “Запустить пилот”; the modal only offers “Понятно”. A director cannot initiate the proposed next step. Restore the approved label and provide an authorized contact route or an honest handoff. Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`.
2. **[P1] Scenario outcomes read as verified proof.** `src/data/story.ts:221–229` says a client replied, a roughly 10 million ruble deal resumed, and another deal returned to work; `src/components/ProofCases.tsx:1–26` frames this as practice. `PRODUCT.md` records no confirmed client results. Label these as synthetic illustrations or remove them until evidence is approved. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.
3. **[P1] Scene 08 breaks continuity from the deal into Dozhim.** `src/chapters/ChapterDozhim.tsx:12–36` and `src/components/DozhimWorkspace.tsx:48–63` show a small, isolated launch card where the storyboard calls for the deal panel expanding into Quick Help. Make the same deal card visibly carry through the transition. Suggested command: `$impeccable animate`.
4. **[P2] Dozhim offers controls that do not respond as expected.** `src/components/DozhimWorkspace.tsx:65–72,117–139` renders History and Deal Context as navigation labels and “Отправить” as a button without a handler. In a presentation, distinguish demonstration controls from functioning interactions or make the permitted local demo action respond. Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`.
5. **[P2] Scene 11 quiz interrupts the promised Quick Help payoff.** `src/chapters/ChapterDozhim.tsx:25–31` asks which deal needs attention after the story has already identified it; `src/components/DealQuiz.tsx:8–35` gives no explanation for a wrong choice. Replace the quiz with the approved follow-up path, or explain the choice if it must stay. Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`.

## Cognitive Load and Emotional Journey

The one-thought-per-scene structure helps comprehension. The 13-item number rail presents more than four choices at once, and quiz and tactic decisions accumulate in the final third. Triage and diagnosis create a credible arc; apparent proof claims and the dead-end CTA weaken trust at its end.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Director or owner:** follows the presentation to the pilot CTA, then finds only an explanatory modal.
- **ROP:** may read the practice cards as measured customer outcomes without supporting evidence.
- **Manager:** can type a question but “Отправить” does nothing; History and Deal Context look like navigation but are static.

- One scene 02 capture showed overlap during a transition; a settled frame is needed before treating it as a stable defect.
